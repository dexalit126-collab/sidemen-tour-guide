Authentic Balinese Lunch & Cooking Class at Juni's Local House

Discover authentic Balinese culture through food at Juni's Local House in the peaceful village of Sidemen, Bali. Whether you choose our Authentic Balinese Lunch or join our hands-on Balinese Cooking Class, you'll experience genuine village life while sharing unforgettable moments with our local family.

Learn how traditional Balinese dishes are prepared using fresh ingredients from our garden and nearby farms. During the cooking class, Juni will guide you through authentic family recipes that have been passed down for generations before you enjoy the delicious meal together.

If you prefer a relaxing cultural experience, simply enjoy our authentic homemade Balinese lunch, served in our traditional family home while learning about Balinese customs, daily life, and local traditions.

More than just a meal or a cooking lesson, this is a meaningful cultural experience where visitors become part of our family, share stories, and discover the true spirit of Sidemen.
