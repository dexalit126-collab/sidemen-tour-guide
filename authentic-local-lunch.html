Authentic Balinese Lunch at Juni's Local House

Escape the tourist crowds and enjoy an authentic Balinese lunch at Juni's Local House in the peaceful village of Sidemen. Our homemade dishes are prepared using fresh local ingredients and traditional family recipes passed down through generations.

This is more than just a meal. You'll be welcomed into our family home, where you can experience genuine Balinese hospitality while learning about local customs, daily life, farming traditions, and the unique culture of Sidemen.

Every dish is freshly prepared with care, allowing you to taste authentic Balinese flavors in a relaxed and welcoming atmosphere. It's the perfect opportunity to connect with local people and experience Bali beyond the usual tourist attractions.

Looking for a more hands-on experience? You can also join our Authentic Balinese Cooking Class, where you'll learn to prepare traditional Balinese dishes before enjoying the meal together.
